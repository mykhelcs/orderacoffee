End-to-End System Operational Flow (Continuous Reactive Loop)

Real-time synchronized lifecycle spanning Customer storefront, Kitchen KDS queue, Driver logistics, and Admin control tower

EVENT BUS: FIRESTORE + MULTI-TIER CACHE

Bidirectional Multi-Actor Synchronization Loop

Changes initiated by any actor immediately propagate to all connected devices in sub-second latency.

● LIVE WEBSOCKET & CACHE READY
☕
Actor 1

Customer Flow

  • 1 Browse & Verify: Real-time menu filtered by category with offline disk cache fallback.
  • 2 Customize: Size, Oat/Almond milk, syrup shots, and special barista instructions.
  • 3 Place & Pay: GCash, Maya, Counter cash, or COD with delivery address validation.
  • 4 Real-time Tracking: Dynamic scooter tracker, 4-stage stepper, and live ETA countdown.
  • 5 Loyalty Reward: Auto-accrue stamps towards free handcrafted drink, re-order in 1-tap.
👨‍🍳
Actor 2

Kitchen KDS & Cashier

  • 1 Instant Ingestion: Audio chime & live ticket injection sorted strictly FIFO by order timestamp.
  • 2 Preparation Countdown: Barista taps "Start Preparing" to trigger the elapsed prep ticker.
  • 3 Stock Deduction: Ingredients automatically deducted from inventory tracking pool.
  • 4 Cash Drawer POS: Handle over-the-counter payments with live change return calculation.
  • 5 Dispatch Alert: Tap "Mark Ready" to alert assigned driver or customer pickup counter.
🛵
Actor 3

Driver Cockpit

  • 1 Online Availability: Driver toggles online status to join active geofence pool.
  • 2 Delivery Pool: View ready orders with pickup distance, destination address, and fee.
  • 3 Claim & Navigate: Accept delivery, pickup hot order, and activate turn-by-turn guidance.
  • 4 Customer Comms: One-tap phone dialer to verify gate access or delivery notes.
  • 5 Handover & COD: Confirm delivery, collect cash (if COD), and loop back to available pool.
🛡️
Actor 4

Admin Management Tower

  • 1 Analytics Monitor: Live gross revenue, net margin, and hourly demand peak graphs.
  • 2 Dispatch Override: Interactive modal to reassign drivers or resolve fulfillment delays.
  • 3 Inventory & Stock: Real-time low-stock alerts (<5 units) with instant +/- increment steppers.
  • 4 Photo Upload: Upload coffee photography directly to Firebase Cloud Storage.
  • 5 Role Security: Promote or revoke Barista, Driver, and Manager RBAC credentials.
Continuous Feedback Loop: Driver location and KDS status stream into Customer tracking screen every 500ms
⚡ Firestore Persistence 💾 Disk Cache (10-min TTL) 🔔 Push Notifications 📱 Role Guards
