End-to-End System Operational Flow (Continuous Reactive Loop)

Real-time synchronized lifecycle spanning Customer storefront, Kitchen KDS queue, Driver logistics, and Admin control tower

☕
Stage 1

Customer Storefront

  • 1 Browse & Search: Live menu filtered by 5 categories with local disk cache.
  • 2 Customize: Size, Oat/Almond milk, sweetness, and special barista instructions.
  • 3 Place & Pay: GCash, Maya, COD, or Counter Cash with address validation.
  • 4 Real-time Tracking: Dynamic scooter tracker, 4-stage stepper, and live ETA.
  • 5 Loyalty Reward: Accrue stamps towards free drink, 1-tap re-order.
⚡
Stage 2

Reactive Event Bus

  • 1 Firestore Streams: Snapshot listeners propagate changes in sub-500ms.
  • 2 Disk Cache: 10-min offline TTL ensures instant startup.
  • 3 Clean Name Resolution: UIDs transformed into customer full names.
  • 4 PayMongo Webhook: Instant payment state verification.
  • 5 FCM Push: High-priority order progress alerts.
👨‍🍳
Stage 3

Kitchen KDS & POS

  • 1 FIFO Ticket Queue: Live incoming orders with prep timer color cues.
  • 2 Start Brewing: Barista advances status to "Preparing" with 1-tap.
  • 3 Stock Deduction: Ingredients automatically deducted from inventory.
  • 4 Cash Drawer POS: Over-the-counter tender with change calculator.
  • 5 Dispatch Alert: "Mark Ready" alerts assigned driver or pickup counter.
🛵
Stage 4

Driver Cockpit

  • 1 Online Availability: Driver joins active geofence pool via toggle.
  • 2 Delivery Pool: View ready orders with pickup distance and fee.
  • 3 Claim & Navigate: Accept delivery, pickup order, activate turn-by-turn.
  • 4 Customer Comms: One-tap phone dialer for gate or delivery notes.
  • 5 Handover & COD: Confirm delivery, collect cash, loop back to pool.
🛡️
Stage 5

Admin Management

  • 1 Analytics Tower: Real-time gross revenue, net margin, hourly demand.
  • 2 Dispatch Override: Reassign drivers or resolve fulfillment delays.
  • 3 Stock Inventory: Real-time low-stock alerts with +/- steppers.
  • 4 Catalog Editor: Update pricing, descriptions, and photo uploads.
  • 5 Role Security: Promote or revoke Barista, Driver, and Admin RBAC.
Continuous Feedback Loop: Driver location and KDS status stream into Customer tracking screen every 500ms
